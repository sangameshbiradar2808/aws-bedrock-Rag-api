Sample Company Handbook (fictional demo content)

IT equipment requests
Employees who need a laptop or other IT equipment should submit a ticket to the IT service desk. Include the business reason, manager approval, and the date the equipment is needed. The IT team confirms availability and provides collection or delivery instructions.

Password and account security
Use a unique password for each work account and enable multi-factor authentication wherever available. Never share passwords or one-time codes. Report suspected phishing messages to the security team and do not open unexpected attachments.

Remote work
Employees working remotely should connect through the company-approved VPN when accessing internal systems. Keep operating systems and security updates current. Do not store company files on personal cloud accounts.

Leave requests
Submit planned leave requests through the HR portal and wait for manager approval before making non-refundable travel arrangements. For urgent absence, notify your manager as soon as practical.

Incident reporting
Report lost equipment, suspicious account activity, or accidental disclosure of company information to the IT service desk or security team immediately. Include the approximate time and a short description of what happened.
